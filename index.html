Born from a love of two kitchens

The blend that started it here.

Culichi-style sushi grew up in Culiacan, Sinaloa, where Japanese technique met Mexican fire. In 2012, chef Eduardo Gonzalez brought it home to Indian School Rd and opened the Valley's original Latin fusion sushi bar.

It is still a family place. Booths and big tables, fight nights on the screens, and a bar that pours micheladas and sake bombers side by side. Featured by Phoenix New Times and Visit Phoenix.
