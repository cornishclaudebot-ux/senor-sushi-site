This Is Señor Sushi

Two Kitchens. One Roll.

Japanese technique meets Sinaloa fire. Culichi-style sushi was born in Culiacan and found its Phoenix home here in 2012, when chef Eduardo Gonzalez opened the Valley's original Latin fusion sushi bar on Indian School Rd.

And it stays a family place. Booths, big tables, fight nights on the screens and a bar pouring micheladas and sake bombers side by side. Featured by Phoenix New Times and Visit Phoenix.
